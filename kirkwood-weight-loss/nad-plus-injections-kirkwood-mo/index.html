    How NAD+ Injection Therapy Works in Kirkwood, MO

    How NAD+ Works in Kirkwood

    NAD+ is nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide. Cells use it to move electrons while making ATP, and enzymes like sirtuins and PARPs burn through it while repairing DNA and handling stress. Older tissue tends to have less. Injected under the skin, NAD+ skips digestion entirely, but researchers are still working out how much of it reaches muscle, brain and other tissue.

    Who NAD+ Injections Are Usually For

    Adults interested in energy, mental clarity, exercise recovery and healthy aging are the usual candidates. People on semaglutide or tirzepatide sometimes add it. It is not a weight loss medication and does not treat any specific disease, and people who are pregnant, breastfeeding, have active cancer or significant liver or kidney disease are generally not candidates.

    NAD+ Dosing and Schedule in Kirkwood

    Small shots, slow push. Many compounded protocols begin around 50 milligrams two or three times a week and rise gradually, often toward 100 milligrams or more, as tolerance builds. Belly and thigh are the usual sites. A slow injection makes a real difference.

    What to Expect in the First Month

    Week one is about technique. Many people feel warmth, flushing, mild nausea or a tight feeling right after injecting, and it usually fades within minutes. By the end of the first month, responders describe more even energy, and plenty of people feel no difference. Doses change based on your notes.

    NAD+ Injection Side Effects in Kirkwood

    Flushing leads the list. Other frequent ones are stinging at the site, nausea, headache and feeling lightheaded. Chest tightness, muscle cramps or a racing heart can happen if the dose is too high or pushed too fast, and these usually improve with a lower dose or slower injection. Rare allergic reactions need emergency care.

    Eligibility and Screening

    The review starts with history and your supplement list. You will be asked about pregnancy, cancer history, liver and kidney health, heart conditions and any NMN, nicotinamide riboside or niacin you already take. Some cases get a metabolic panel. Upload recent labs if you have them.

    NAD+ Injections Compared to Other Options in Kirkwood

    IV infusions are the usual comparison. IV NAD+ puts more in at once, though sessions often last hours and the discomfort during the drip is a common complaint. The nasal spray is needle-free. Oral precursors like NMN and nicotinamide riboside are supplements that help the body make its own NAD+.
