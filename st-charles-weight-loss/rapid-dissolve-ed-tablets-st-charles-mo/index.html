    How Rapid Dissolve Tablets Work in St. Charles

    Most rapid dissolve ED tablets contain sildenafil, tadalafil or occasionally both. Some medication may absorb through the lining of the mouth, and the rest is swallowed with saliva. Arousal is still required.

    Dosing and Timing in St. Charles

    Rapid dissolve tablets are taken as needed, usually 15 to 60 minutes before sex depending on the formula. Let it sit under your tongue until it is gone, typically one to three minutes, without chewing or swallowing it whole. Hold off on eating or drinking until then. One dose per 24 hours.     Plenty of men notice an effect on the first or second use. Getting the timing right can take a few tries.     Side Effects in St. Charles

    Side effects follow the active ingredient. Headache, flushing, stuffy nose, indigestion and dizziness are common with both sildenafil and tadalafil formulas, and tadalafil versions can also cause back or muscle aches the next day. The base can irritate the mouth slightly. Seek emergency care for an erection over four hours or sudden vision or hearing changes. Rare, but serious.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Nitrates rule these tablets out, including nitroglycerin, isosorbide and poppers. Riociguat does as well. A heart attack or stroke in the past six months, unstable chest pain, uncontrolled blood pressure or severe liver or kidney disease all need careful review and may make the tablets unsafe. Alpha blockers like tamsulosin and drugs like ketoconazole or ritonavir raise drug levels. Food allergies get asked about too.

    Rapid Dissolve Tablets Compared to Other Options in St. Charles

    Compounded rapid dissolve tablets are not FDA-approved. FDA-approved generic tablets use the same drugs and carry decades of research, so many providers try them first. Troches dissolve more slowly. Troches go in the cheek and dissolve over 10 to 20 minutes. Daily chewables offer steady low-dose coverage instead.
